Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 27, 2026 • Publisher: NovaGrid Labs (TableSnap Pro Browser Extension)

1. Overview & Zero-Data-Collection Principle

NovaGrid Labs ("we", "our", "us") builds client-side browser extensions, including TableSnap Pro. We do not operate tracking servers, do not sell user data, and never collect, transmit, or store the contents of webpages or tables you inspect or export.

2. How Browser Permissions Are Used

  • activeTab & scripting: Triggered strictly when you open the TableSnap Pro popup on a webpage to detect, highlight, and convert HTML/ARIA tables into downloadable files (.XLSX, .CSV, .JSON, Markdown) locally inside your browser memory.
  • storage: Used exclusively on your local device (chrome.storage.local) to remember your formatting preferences, daily free export counter, and Pro license activation state.
  • Host Permission (https://api.lemonsqueezy.com/*): Used solely when a Pro customer enters a license key, sending only the license key string and an anonymous random browser instance identifier over HTTPS to Lemon Squeezy's official License API to verify validity.

3. Payments & Merchant of Record

Optional Pro upgrades are processed securely by our Merchant of Record, Lemon Squeezy. NovaGrid Labs never receives or stores your credit card details.

4. Third-Party Sharing & Analytics

TableSnap Pro contains zero third-party analytics SDKs, zero advertising scripts, and zero browsing history trackers.
