Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 27, 2026 • Publisher: NovaGrid Labs (Lisbon, Portugal) • Products Covered: TableSnap Pro & NovaGrid Browser Utilities

1. Strict Client-Side & Zero-Data-Collection Architecture

NovaGrid Labs ("we", "our", "us") builds privacy-first browser software, including TableSnap Pro. We do not operate data-harvesting servers, do not use third-party analytics or ad-tracking SDKs, and never collect, read, transmit, or store the contents of webpages, documents, or tables you inspect or export.

2. How Browser Extension Permissions Are Used

  • activeTab & scripting: Executed strictly on demand when you explicitly click the extension icon on an active tab to detect, highlight, and convert HTML/ARIA tables into downloadable files (.xlsx, .csv, .json, Markdown) locally inside your browser memory.
  • storage (Local Storage): Used exclusively on your own device (chrome.storage.local) to store your formatting toggles, daily free export counter, and Pro license activation status.
  • Host Permission (https://api.lemonsqueezy.com/*): Used solely when a Pro license holder activates or validates a license key. Only the license key string and an anonymous local browser instance identifier are transmitted over encrypted HTTPS to Lemon Squeezy's official License API.

3. Payments, Billing & Merchant of Record

Optional Pro upgrades (Monthly or One-Time Lifetime licenses) are processed securely by our authorized Merchant of Record, Lemon Squeezy. NovaGrid Labs never receives, processes, or stores credit card numbers or banking credentials.

4. Data Retention & User Control

Because all extension preferences and license states are stored locally in your browser, uninstalling the extension or clearing extension storage immediately removes all local data from your device.
